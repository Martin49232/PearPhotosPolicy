Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Pear Photos application (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices. This service is provided "AS IS".

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not gather, collect, or transmit your personal data to any external servers operated by us. Pear Photos is designed to act as a local client that interacts directly with Apple's iCloud Shared Albums.

  • iCloud Shared Albums: When you provide a public iCloud Shared Album URL, the Application connects directly to Apple's servers to fetch and download the photos and videos contained within that specific album.
  • Local Storage: Downloaded media is stored directly and exclusively on your local device (within the standard Android MediaStore).

2. Third-Party Services and Billing

The Application utilizes third-party services to function and process purchases:

  • Google Play Billing: The Application offers premium features via in-app purchases handled entirely by the Google Play Billing Library. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card details or personal payment information. Google Play's privacy policy governs the payment process.
  • Apple iCloud: To view albums, the Application fetches data from public iCloud endpoints. Apple's privacy policy applies to data retrieved from their services.

3. Security

We value your trust in our Application. Since we do not collect your personal data or route it through our own servers, the primary security of your media relies on your device's security and the security of the public iCloud links you choose to add. Please remember that public iCloud links can be accessed by anyone who has the URL.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
